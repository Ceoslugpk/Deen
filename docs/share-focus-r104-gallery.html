How Focus zoom works: Pinch with two fingers to enlarge or return to fit. While magnified, drag to pan instead of turning pages. At fit, swipe right for the next page. Unlock remains fixed at the safe top-right. Unlock/re-entry, rotation, leaving the reader or a different printed page resets the transient magnification; saved reader settings remain unchanged.

Sharing: The requested button opens the platform share flow. Browsers without Web Share use their clipboard API and show an honest copy confirmation. Dismissal is silent; errors leave the selectable URL and Google Play action available. The QR is bundled, with no QR-service request.
